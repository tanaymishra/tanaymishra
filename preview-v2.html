how this page works

no javascript, no external images, no font CDNs. github strips <script>, <style> and every event handler before your browser sees this file, and the SVGs are served under default-src 'none' so they cannot fetch anything either. the pixel art is a few hundred <rect> elements placed at build time, and the only thing moving is SMIL animation, the one thing github's sanitizer leaves alive.

the numbers are real, pulled from the GraphQL API, including the ones that make me look smaller. 3 public stars. 3,549 of my 3,830 contributions this year are private: the green squares lie.

the project statuses are read from the Zyloris source, so CONCEPT means it is genuinely still a concept. the visitor counter is hardcoded to 1337. you knew that.
